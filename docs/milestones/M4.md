# Milestone 4 — 2D Navier–Stokes solver and validation

## Plan (written before the implementation)

**Governing equations.** Solve incompressible Navier–Stokes with unit density,
`u_t + (u·∇)u = -∇p + ν∇²u + f`, `div(u)=0`, on the MAC grid from M1. Temperature/dye is
advected and optionally diffused independently.

**Discretisation.** Use the existing semi-Lagrangian/MacCormack/BFECC advection, explicit
forces, backward-Euler viscosity `(I-νdtL)u_new=u_old`, and the M3 pressure projection. The
viscous operator uses `α=νdt/h²`, ghost/periodic boundary stencils, and matrix-free diagonal
PCG. Automatic `dt` is CFL-based with a cell-crossing cap; fixed `dt` is used for validation.

**Stability.** Implicit diffusion is unconditionally stable. Advection remains
semi-Lagrangian stable for large CFL but loses accuracy; I keep CFL near one. The operator
split has a measured first-order temporal error, so unconditional stability is not treated
as an accuracy proof.

**Correctness tests.** Check the diffusion operator against an explicit padded Laplacian,
verify SPD on the free unknowns, test periodic Taylor–Green energy decay and wall damping,
then run the full solver against analytic Taylor–Green energy and the parabolic plane
Poiseuille profile. Test divergence, CFL reporting, body-force signs, scalar diffusion, and
final-time handling. Strict mypy and ruff must pass before the commit.

## What was built

* `src/mackerel/diffuse.py`: implicit velocity and scalar diffusion with periodic, zero-
  gradient, prescribed-normal and no-slip ghost modes. The duplicated periodic MAC seam is
  reduced for the solve and restored afterwards. `DiffusionResult` reports PCG iterations and
  residual.
* `src/mackerel/simulate.py`: `SimulationConfig`, `Simulation`, `StepInfo`, CFL/fixed time
  stepping, force and temperature-buoyancy hooks, scalar transport, and `run()`/`run_until()`.
* `validation/taylor_green.py`: full-solver energy decay, effective decay rates, scheme
  comparison, and fixed-`dt` refinement data/figures in `outputs/m4/`.
* `validation/poiseuille.py`: body-force channel steady profiles and observed spatial order.
* `tests/test_diffuse.py` and `tests/test_simulate.py`: 26 focused tests, including explicit
  stencil comparisons and integration checks.

## Equations and algorithms

The viscous stage is `(I-αh²L)u^{***}=u^{**}` with `α=νdt/h²`; it is solved independently for
`u` and `v` by preconditioned CG. For a no-slip tangential edge, `u_ghost=2u_wall-u_0` gives
an edge diagonal `1+5α` and a right-hand-side contribution `2αu_wall`. Normal prescribed faces
are eliminated and their known values are moved to the RHS. The scalar equation is the same
with its own diffusivity and zero-gradient/periodic walls.

The complete step is advect → force → diffuse → project. The pressure RHS remains
`div(u^{***})/dt`, followed by `u^{n+1}=u^{***}-dt∇p`. The Taylor–Green reference is
`E(t)=E_0 exp(-4(2π)²νt)`. The channel reference is `u=f y(H-y)/(2ν)`.

| scheme | final energy | analytic energy | relative error | max divergence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| semi-Lagrangian | 0.16484446 | 0.18171141 | −9.282e−2 | 1.92e−10 |

| BFECC (corrected) | 0.18015592 | 0.18171141 | −8.560e−3 | 1.89e−10 |

The BFECC row was remeasured after fixing a staggered-grid bug in its final `v` sweep.
The legacy control used the `u` offsets `(-h,-h/2)` for that sweep and reproduced the old
`-22.9431%` energy error (`E=.140021137`). The corrected `(-h/2,-h)` sweep gives `-0.8560%`
(`E=.180155916`). The controlled variants below all use the same 64² full split step:

| BFECC variant | relative energy error |
|---|---:|
| legacy `v` offsets | −22.9431% |
| corrected, limiter on | −0.8560% |
| corrected, limiter off | −0.7849% |
| corrected, old-field reverse trajectory | −0.8585% |
| corrected, wrong correction sign | −16.7659% |

Thus the dominant cause was the `v`-face sampling offset, not the limiter or the choice of
reverse trajectory. The scalar BFECC path already used the appropriate component offsets;
its separate rotation tests do not exercise this velocity-only bug. The wrong-sign control is
included to show that the correction sign matters, but it is not the production path. Raw
controls are regenerated by `python -m validation.bfecc` in `bfecc_investigation.{json,csv,png}`.

The lower MacCormack error is why it is used for the refinement reference; no result is
relabelled as exact. A fixed-`dt` MacCormack sweep at `32²/64²/128²` gave relative errors
`−6.913e−3/−7.787e−3/−8.010e−3`: temporal splitting dominates this particular comparison,
so the fitted slope is −0.11, not a claimed second-order spatial rate. Halving `dt` at `64²`
reduced the error (relative to the continuum energy) from −3.42% at `dt=.01` to −0.70% at
`dt=.00125`, consistent with a temporal contribution.

Diffusion-only periodic Taylor–Green over `t=.5`, `64²`, `dt=.005` matched the analytic energy
ratio to `7.3e−5` relative at `ν=.001` and `9.94e−4` at `ν=.01`; the operator versus an
explicit Laplacian was `4.44e−16` in the direct ghost/Neumann checks. Plane Poiseuille with

## Numerical tradeoffs

Backward Euler costs a linear solve but removes the severe explicit viscous time-step
restriction; at small `α` PCG usually needs only a few iterations. Eliminating prescribed
faces is slightly more bookkeeping than pinning them, but preserves symmetry and makes CG
valid. The split step is simple and modular, yet its temporal error is visible in Taylor–Green
energy even when divergence is tiny. Semi-Lagrangian advection is robust but diffusive;
corrected schemes reduce that diffusion for smooth velocity fields, while their nonlinear
limiters can still remove energy and are not conservative. A fixed-`dt` refinement study is
necessary because a fixed-CFL study changes both spatial and temporal error at once.

## Task 2 follow-up — independent spatial and temporal convergence

The earlier fixed-`dt` energy sweep was not a pure spatial study. The new reproducible driver
is `python -m validation.convergence`; raw results and plots are in
`outputs/m4/convergence/`. All cases use MacCormack, `ν=.01`, CG tolerance `1e-9`, and the
same analytic Taylor–Green definitions.

For the **spatial study**, `dt=6.25e-5`, `t=.05` (800 steps per case) was fixed before running
at 32²/64²/128². The primary error is velocity L2 against the analytic decaying field:

| grid | velocity L2 | energy relative error | pairwise spatial order (velocity) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 32² | 1.019753e−3 | +1.969913e−4 | — |
| 64² | 2.710461e−4 | +6.284522e−6 | 1.91161 |
| 128² | 8.416203e−5 | −4.144710e−5 | 1.68730 |

The least-squares spatial velocity order is **1.79945**. The energy-only fit is `1.12439`,
with pairwise values `4.97018` and `−2.72139` because the signed energy error changes near
zero; it is not presented as a clean spatial order. The maximum divergence in these three
runs was `4.8904e−12`.

For the **temporal study**, the grid was fixed at 128² and `t=.2`:

| dt | energy relative error | velocity L2 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| .004 | −1.279267e−2 | 4.784036e−3 |
| .002 | −6.406205e−3 | 2.424046e−3 |
| .001 | −3.182401e−3 | 1.233108e−3 |
| .0005 | −1.562323e−3 | 6.348489e−4 |

The fitted temporal orders are **1.01100** for absolute relative energy error and **0.97163**
for velocity L2. A symmetric P/2–D/2–A–D/2–P/2 Strang control at 64² gave an energy order of
`1.01884`, compared with `1.02222` for Lie at the same four steps (`dt=.008/.004/.002/.001`).
At `dt=.001`, for example, Lie was `−.00298095` and Strang `−.00304022`; Strang was not adopted
because this measured control did not improve the error. Its implementation is validation-only
and assumes force-free, scalar-free, obstacle-free Taylor–Green flow.
